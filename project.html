Man cannot live on work alone; here is an incomplete list of the many little projects and tinkerings by which I avoid it.

CONJUGATOR

A website I built on a coding course to help me learn Spanish verb conjugations. Simply guess the correct conjugation, then click the blank space to reveal it. Currently includes present, participle, preterite, and imperfect tenses, and works on mobile or browser.

MODS

Namelist mods created for Paradox's sci-fi grand strategy Stellaris. Each namelist includes thousands of potential character names and hundreds of ship and planet names.

Earthseed

Earthseed began with an eco-socialist themed namelist for a human nation with the Lost Colony origin, drawing on Andean, indigenous American, and Pacific Islander influences. Inspired by Sid Meier's Alpha Centauri, the mod soon expanded to include five distinct namelists, each based around a different concept for a lost human colony.

MegaCorp Namelist

Originally part of the Earthseed mod above, I split it off as its own thing. Designed for MegaCorp players, it ideates unique forward-focused brand concepts for your planets, ships, and co-creatives. Wow your brand partners with planets like Goliath Gravitonic and Ranger Rick's Family Funworld; discover exciting new opportunities aboard the LLC Loss Adjustor; or hit the hyper-squash court with co-entrepeneurs like Ted VibraLife-Kravitz and Trish Starforge-Wu. RANDOM GENERATORS

I love names: inventing them, hearing them, forgetting them. I especially enjoy making random generators, for tabletop games, stories, or, more commonly, just for fun. Here are a few of my favourites:

  • English Town Names. The UK leads the world in weird little towns you can only access by rail replacement bus under a harvest moon. Get your own here!
  • Gubernatorial Names. Originating with a tweet about oddly-named US state governors, I expanded it with 100% genuine American Civil War era names.
  • What's My Architecture? Generate random, somewhat eclectic buildings for your needs. Created for a homebrew Blades in the Dark campaign.
  • Starship Names. Somewhat human-centric; served as the basis for my Stellaris namelist mods.
  • Art Style Generator. Need a new hot movement for all the cool kids to be so over? Look no further!
