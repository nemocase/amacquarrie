Who I Am

I’m an award-winning writer with over a decade in film, theatre, audio, and narrative games. With a keen understanding of story, a knack for dialogue, and experience on over a dozen published projects, I specialise in bringing to life unusual people and fascinating worlds.

Since 2019 I’ve worked for Naomi Alderman on her mobile series Zombies, Run! as a writer and script editor.
