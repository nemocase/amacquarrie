<p>I’m an award-winning writer with over a decade in film, theatre, audio, and narrative games. With a keen understanding of story, a knack for dialogue, and experience on over a dozen published projects, I specialise in bringing to life unusual people and fascinating worlds.</p>

<p>Since 2019 I’ve worked for Naomi Alderman on her mobile series Zombies, Run! as a writer and script editor, developing new storylines across three seasons and numerous side projects. Prior to that, I directed, produced, and wrote (sometimes all three) multiple plays including Gulf (2014) at the Camden People’s Theatre; and acted as a script editor for Chichester Festival Theatre’s Out of the Archive Project. In 2017 I completed an MA Scriptwriting at Goldsmiths, where I collaborated with filmmakers from around the world and organised the end-of-year degree showcase.</p>

<p>In 2026, I was a pre-selector for the Cornwall Film Festival. As a critic, my essays have been published in Rock Paper Shotgun, Eurogamer, the New Socialist, and Fanbyte, and on the film and TV blog I ran from 2015 - 2020. With the same critical eye that makes me a good editor, I enjoy taking stories apart and seeing what makes them tick (or jams the gears). As a history grad who draws much of my inspiration from the past, I’m always interested in how we choose to depict it on screen, and what that says about our present.</p>

<p>As well as writing for Zombies, Run!, I’m currently producing a radio play based on H.G. Wells' <i>The Island of Dr Moreau</i>; writing short stories; and co-hosting the <a href="https://open.spotify.com/show/3CB6RVo5cd87UtCV0KyfKE?si=e0158291f2654e8a">Max Peril</a> film podcast.</p>
